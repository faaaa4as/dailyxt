## Why
I wanted a way to stay in touch with the ecosystems and news that I like.
I love my X4 and wanted to see what use other than books I could get out of it.
I want to reduce my phone screen time; the goal is to read nothing from my phone until I arrive at work.

## What
Each run gathers content from multiple sources and transforms, or does not transform, it as described below:
- Hand picked Substacks -> untouched, pure article to EPUB, images included.
- YouTube playlists -> summaries for videos that were not there last run.
- Hacker News -> fetch main page, rank each entry based on my interests, and summarize.

Deliver to X4 under Crosspoint.

## Problem
Spend less time scrolling X; Hacker News often covers the most relevant X posts I see in a day.
Spend less time on YouTube; spend a little time scrolling, gather videos that pique my interest, then read summaries tomorrow.
Substack on e-ink is just cool.
